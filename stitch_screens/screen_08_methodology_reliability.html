L_t = σ(k⋅ΔRRR) × W_t × E_t
Second Innings Formulation (Chasing Dynamics) Equation 2.1

Pressure state L_t ∈ [0, 100] is dynamically computed at ball index t as a logistic sigmoidal transformation of Required Run Rate (RRR) shifted against par velocity, modulated by wickets lost and non-linear endgame horizon compression.

L_t = σ(k_1 ⋅ RRR_t - c) × &Bigl(1 + \frac{\text{wickets\_lost}_t}{10}\Bigr) × \Phi_{\text{endgame}}(B_{\text{rem}})
k_1 = 0.42 (Sigmoid slope)
c = 3.65 (Par shift)
Φ(B) = 1 + e^(-B_rem / 12)

To prevent small-sample volatility (e.g. a batter striking at 240% over 8 balls) from distorting war room valuations, raw observations are shrunk toward population priors proportional to sample evidence volume.

θ_shrunk = \frac{n}{n + K} ⋅ \bar{x}_{\text{observed}} + \frac{K}{n + K} ⋅ μ_{\text{prior}}

Sample Disparities

Pronounced sample volume asymmetries exist between seasoned franchise players (60+ IPL caps) and emerging or associate players (limited to regional T20Is). Cross-cohort comparisons must refer directly to the SSSI index.

Competition Quality Deltas

International bilateral series exhibit variance in bowler speed distributions and venue par averages. An IPL environment multiplier (0.92 to 1.08) is applied based on historical ICC vs IPL transition indices.

A player utilized as an anchor in domestic circuits who is targeted as a death-overs finisher in Rajasthan Royals schema will exhibit role dissonance. Metrics reflect past usage, not coach-assigned target functions.

Injury & Biomechanical Recovery

Historical releases do not automatically capture post-surgical arm speeds or altered bowling actions. Medical clearances and high-speed motion capture must supersede purely retrospective statistical signals.
