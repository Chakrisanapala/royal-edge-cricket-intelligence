## 2. Dynamic Match Pressure Engine Verification

### Theoretical Formulation vs. Implementation

Static phase assumptions (e.g. labeling all overs 16–20 as high pressure) are rejected. A run-chase requiring 12 runs off 30 balls with 8 wickets in hand in over 17 represents negligible pressure. Pressure is evaluated delivery-by-delivery based on dynamic match leverage.

#### 1. Second Innings (The Run Chase)
* **Dynamic Required Run Rate (RRR):**
  $$\text{RRR}_t = \frac{\text{Runs Needed}_t}{\text{Balls Remaining}_t} \times 6$$
* **Sigmoid Leverage Response:**
  $$P_{\text{RRR}} = \frac{100}{1 + e^{-0.45 \times (\text{RRR} - 9.0)}}$$
  * *Verification in Code (`src/pressure_engine.py` Line 142):* Calibrated so par T20 RRR (9.0) corresponds to the sigmoid inflection point ($P=50$). An RRR of 12.0 scales to $P=79.4$, and RRR $> 14.0$ scales to $P > 90.0$.
* **Resource Depletion Multiplier:**
  $$M_{\text{wickets}} = 1 + \max(0, (5 - \text{Wickets in Hand}) \times 0.08)$$
  * *Verification:* Evaluates wickets remaining. When 8 wickets are lost (2 in hand), multiplier reaches $1 + (5 - 2) \times 0.08 = 1.24\times$.
* **Endgame Proximity Boost:**
  * When $\text{Balls Remaining} \le 24$ and $\text{Runs Needed} \le 2.2 \times \text{Balls Remaining}$, pressure is guaranteed at:
    $$\text{Endgame Pressure} = 70.0 + (24 - \text{Balls Remaining})$$
  * A final-over delivery (6 balls remaining) carries an automatic baseline leverage of $70 + 18 = 88.0$ (Extreme Tier).
* **Knockout Context Adjustment:** $+8.0$ flat leverage points in playoffs/finals.

#### 2. First Innings (Target Setting)
* **Collapse Recovery Trigger:**
  * If $\ge 3$ wickets fall in overs 1–10 (or score $<40$ at 3 down), delivery leverage is immediately elevated to $65.0$ (High Pressure Tier).
* **Death-Over Boundary Maximization:**
  * In overs 16–20 with $\ge 5$ wickets in hand, pressure scales linearly from $65.0$ to $77.0$ reflecting urgent acceleration imperatives.

#### 3. Empirical Tier Distribution Across 200,999 Deliveries:
* **Low Pressure ($<35$):** 46,248 deliveries (23.01%)
* **Moderate Pressure ($35-59$):** 64,300 deliveries (31.99%)
* **High Pressure ($60-79$):** 61,080 deliveries (30.39%)
* **Extreme Pressure ($\ge 80$):** 29,371 deliveries (14.61%)
* **Combined High + Extreme Pressure:** 45,951 deliveries (22.86%) under strict qualifying pressure filters.

## 3. Mathematical Index Architecture

### Batting Pressure Reliability Index (BPRI)

$$\text{BPRI}_{\text{raw}} = 0.30 \cdot P_{\text{baseline}} + 0.20 \cdot P_{\text{SR}} + 0.15 \cdot P_{\text{control}} + 0.15 \cdot P_{\text{chase}} + 0.10 \cdot P_{\text{death}} + 0.10 \cdot P_{\text{consistency}}$$

| Pillar | Weight | Description | Implementation Metric |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Baseline Differential** | **30%** | Lift under pressure relative to player normal | $(SR_{\text{press}} - SR_{\text{norm}}) \cdot 0.6 + (Avg_{\text{press}} - Avg_{\text{norm}}) \cdot 0.4$ |
| **2. High-Pressure SR** | **20%** | Raw scoring pace in leveraged scenarios | Min-max scaled High-Pressure Strike Rate |
| **3. Control & Boundary** | **15%** | Boundary frequency vs dot ball avoidance | $(\text{Boundary \% under pressure}) - (\text{Dot \% under pressure})$ |
| **4. Chase Performance** | **15%** | Execution in innings 2 pressure chases | Innings 2 High-Pressure SR and Average |
| **5. Death Execution** | **10%** | Lethality in overs 16–20 | Death-over Strike Rate and Boundary % |
| **6. Consistency** | **10%** | Regularity across matches | Inverted match-to-match Strike Rate standard deviation |

### Bowling Pressure Reliability Index (BOWPRI)

$$\text{BOWPRI}_{\text{raw}} = 0.30 \cdot P_{\text{baseline}} + 0.20 \cdot P_{\text{econ}} + 0.15 \cdot P_{\text{wickets}} + 0.15 \cdot P_{\text{prevention}} + 0.10 \cdot P_{\text{death}} + 0.10 \cdot P_{\text{consistency}}$$

| Pillar | Weight | Description | Implementation Metric |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Baseline Differential** | **30%** | Resistance to economy inflation under strain | $Econ_{\text{norm}} - Econ_{\text{press}}$ (positive = economy improves) |
| **2. Pressure Economy** | **20%** | Run suppression under high leverage | Inverted High-Pressure Economy Rate |
| **3. Wicket Effectiveness** | **15%** | Breakthrough capability under pressure | Inverted legal deliveries per wicket under pressure |
| **4. Choke & Dot Impact** | **15%** | Pressure generation via dot balls | $(\text{Dot \% under pressure}) - (\text{Boundary conceded \%})$ |
| **5. Death Execution** | **10%** | Overs 16–20 defensive containment | Inverted death overs economy & wickets per 100 balls |
| **6. Consistency** | **10%** | Match-to-match stability | Inverted standard deviation of match economy rates |

## 4. Regularization & Uncertainty Framework

### 1. Empirical Bayesian Shrinkage
To prevent small-sample distortions from dominating leaderboards, raw scores are regularized against population mean priors:
$$\text{Index}_{\text{final}} = \left(\frac{N}{N + K}\right) \cdot \text{Index}_{\text{raw}} + \left(\frac{K}{N + K}\right) \cdot \mu_{\text{prior}}$$
* **Batting Prior:** $K_{\text{bat}} = 60.0$, $\mu_{\text{bat}} = 50.0$.
* **Bowling Prior:** $K_{\text{bowl}} = 50.0$, $\mu_{\text{bowl}} = 50.0$.
* **Qualification Thresholds:** Batters $\ge 10$ innings & $\ge 75$ pressure balls; Bowlers $\ge 10$ innings & $\ge 60$ legal balls.

### 2. Non-Parametric Cluster Bootstrap (1,000 Iterations)
* Measurement uncertainty is established through 1,000 resamples with replacement clustered at the match level.
* Yields true **95% Bootstrap Confidence Intervals** $[CI_{\text{lower}}, CI_{\text{upper}}]$ and Standard Errors.
* *Example (Ashutosh Sharma):* HP SR 168.62, 95% CI: $[148.67, 189.74]$, Standard Error: $10.83$.

### 3. Sample Size Saturation Index (SSSI %)
* **Definition:**
  $$\text{SSSI} = \min\left(100.0, \frac{\text{Pressure Deliveries}}{\text{Target Saturation Threshold}} \times 100.0\right)$$
* *Target Thresholds:* 250 pressure balls faced (batters); 200 pressure legal balls bowled (bowlers).
* **Scientific Caveat:** SSSI is strictly an empirical indicator of sample volume adequacy. It is **NOT** a probability of match victory or a frequentist confidence percentage.
