### 1. The Core Analytical Dichotomy

A common pitfall in sports analytics is conflating **mathematical performance in a historical dataset** with **real-world regulatory acquisition feasibility**.

In this challenge, the supplied dataset contains both Indian Premier League (IPL) matches and global T20 Internationals (T20Is) across 2022–2026. Consequently, players from non-IPL nations (e.g., Pakistan, Ireland) appear in the delivery logs and qualify mathematically.

The Rajasthan Royals Analytics Team must maintain **three distinct, uncompromised evaluation layers**:
1. **Layer 1: Pure Statistical Excellence:** Evaluates players strictly on delivery-level evidence without political or contractual filtering.
2. **Layer 2: IPL Regulatory & Auction Eligibility:** Applies BCCI IPL Player Regulations, international availability, and bilateral agreements.
3. **Layer 3: Rajasthan Royals Tactical Suitability:** Assesses roster composition, Indian core balance, Sawai Mansingh Stadium pitch profiles, and overseas slot economy.
